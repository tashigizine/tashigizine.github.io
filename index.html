About

Heart of Shigure is a fan-made zine in celebration of Captain Tashigi and all the people who love her.

Through art, writing, cosplay and merchandise, we hope to bring to life a project that showcases her many adventures, relationships and unforgettable moments while bringing the community together.

Applications & Guidelines

View complete guidelines

Artist

Artists will be expected to create one size A5 portrait artwork, or a size A4 spread. We will be looking at anatomy, backgrounds, piece composition, and coloring in your portfolios. One Piece pieces are encouraged but not mandatory.

Merch Artist

We are looking for merch artists to create a variety of merch! The results of the interest check will determine if the merch will be digital or physical. We would love to see merch mock-ups in your portfolio. While we would prefer to see One Piece examples, it is not mandatory.

Writer

We are looking for writers! We encourage One Piece samples in your portfolio, but not mandatory. Writers will be expected to create a piece approximately 1500-2000 words in length. We will be looking for proper grammar, characterization, and story cohesion in your samples. Samples must NOT exceed 1500 words in length.

Cosplayer

Cosplayers are expected to submit one photo preferably from a photoshoot for the zine. One Piece cosplays are encouraged but not mandatory.

FAQ

What is a fanzine?

A fanzine, or zine, is an art project focused on a theme made by a team of people (usually fans!). It normally includes fanart, fanfic, merch, and more!

What's this zine about?

This zine is all about Captain Tashigi, our favorite marine swordswoman! We want to have pieces of her with her friends (and/or lovers), doing cool sword stuff, experiencing the horrors, being nerdy, etc. All Tashigi, all the time~

Is this for charity or for profit?

This will be determined by the volume of sign-ups!

Is shipping allowed?

Shipping will be allowed, and we aim to have it cover roughly a 30% of the content in the zine. All ships are welcome. We love LOVE!

Is NSFW allowed?

NSFW won't be allowed in this project.

Is AI allowed?

NO. No AI will be allowed in the creation of ANY part of this project.
